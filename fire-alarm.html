Annual inspection & testing (S536)

In plain language: the Ontario Fire Code requires an annual inspection and testing of the fire alarm system to the S536 standard. Our certified fire alarm technicians carry out that annual work and give you a written report with a deficiency list. Monthly tests are typically the owner's or management's duty — we focus on the annual inspection and can include annual emergency lighting in the same visit.

We test and inspect:

  • the control unit and annunciators
  • initiating devices: smoke and heat detectors, pull stations, sprinkler flow and supervisory switches
  • audible and visual signal devices
  • batteries and power supplies
  • monitoring signal transmission

You receive a written inspection report with a deficiency list.

Verification (S537)

Verification of new systems, and of existing systems after additions, alterations or device replacements, for example after a suite renovation, tenant fit-out or panel replacement. It is coordinated with the electrical work, so there is one contractor for both.

Periodic testing & repairs

Monthly and periodic tests, trouble and supervisory signal troubleshooting, device replacement, wiring faults, battery replacement, and correction of deficiencies from any inspection report (ours or another firm's).

Fire alarm installation

Our certified fire alarm technicians install fire alarm systems in new spaces and extend or modify existing systems, for example during a renovation, suite upgrade or tenant fit-out.

  • New fire alarm system installation
  • Additions and device changes to existing systems
  • Wiring coordinated with the electrical work, so there is one contractor for both
  • S537 verification once the installation is complete

Frequently asked questions

How often does a fire alarm need to be inspected in Ontario?

Every fire alarm system must be inspected and tested annually to the S536 standard, plus the periodic checks set out in the Ontario Fire Code.

What's the difference between S536 and S537?

S536 is the annual inspection and testing of an existing system. S537 is the verification of a new system, or of an existing system after additions or alterations.

Do you fix deficiencies found by another inspection company?

Yes. Send us the report and we will quote the corrections.

Will residents be disturbed?

We provide advance notices for residents and schedule testing windows with management to keep disruption low.

Do you provide a report for our records?

Yes. You receive a written inspection report with a deficiency list, and we make the logbook entries for your building records.

Do you serve Vaughan and York Region?

Yes. We inspect and repair fire alarm systems in condos, rental buildings, offices, plazas and industrial units across Vaughan and York Region. We coordinate with building management and, when the building's fire safety plan requires it, with Vaughan Fire & Rescue Service or the local fire department.

Who does the monthly fire alarm tests?

Monthly tests are typically the owner's or management's duty under the Ontario Fire Code. Our visit covers the annual S536 inspection and testing; we can include annual emergency lighting in the same visit.
