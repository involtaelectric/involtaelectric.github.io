Common questions from property managers

Do you do annual fire alarm inspections (S536)?

Yes. Our certified fire alarm technicians carry out annual fire alarm inspection and testing to the S536 standard required under the Ontario Fire Code, with a written report and deficiency list for your records. See fire alarm inspections.

Do you offer maintenance agreements?

Yes. We can bundle your annual fire alarm and emergency lighting inspections with scheduled electrical maintenance, so one contractor looks after the building. Ask us about an agreement for your property.

Do you pull ESA permits and provide certificates?

Yes. We are a licensed electrical contractor (ECRA/ESA #7016967). We take out ESA permits for notifiable electrical work and provide the ESA Certificate of Acceptance once the work passes inspection.

How do you handle suite access and residents?

We provide notices for fire alarm testing and coordinate suite access with management for in-suite devices, so residents know when to expect us.
