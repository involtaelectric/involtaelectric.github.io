About Involta Electric

A licensed Ontario electrical contractor serving Vaughan, York Region and the Greater Toronto Area.

Our mission

Our mission is to empower our clients by providing exceptional electrical services that exceed expectations. We prioritize understanding each client's unique needs, budget and demands.

Through innovative solutions, using the latest technology and equipment, we simplify complex electrical systems and make the process convenient and worry-free. We aim to be a trusted resource, guiding clients through every step and making sure they are completely satisfied.

Involta Electric Ltd. is an ECRA/ESA-licensed electrical contractor serving property managers, condo boards and commercial buildings across the Greater Toronto Area. Our licensed team looks after every project from the first estimate through to completion.
